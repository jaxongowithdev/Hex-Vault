APP NAME
Larder Haven

SUBTITLE
Map your pantry shelves

KEYWORDS
pantry,kitchen,larder,spice,grocery,stock,jar,shelf,food,restock,organize,offline

PROMOTION
Catalog jars, spices, and dry goods by shelf. See what is low before you shop. Private, offline, and built for a real kitchen.

DESCRIPTION
Larder Haven is a quiet kitchen catalog for people who cook from what they already have.

Map every larder in the house — the spice drawer, the baking bay, the freezer door, the overflow cupboard — then file each staple where it actually lives. When you wonder whether you still have smoked paprika or a second bag of oats, search once instead of opening every door.

What you can do
• Create named larders with a shelf mark, kitchen zone, and bay
• File staples by aisle: grains, spices, oils, canned, baking, tea, and more
• Mark stock as Fresh, Opened, Low, or Expired
• Attach a label photo from the camera or your album
• Pin weekly staples you reach for again and again
• Reshelve a jar from one larder to another and keep a short note
• See a kitchen snapshot: how many larders are in use, which aisles are fullest
• Export or restore the whole catalog as a JSON file

Why it is different
Larder Haven is not a shopping list and not a generic box inventory. It is a pantry map. The language, layout, and categories are written for a working kitchen: zones instead of rooms, bays instead of warehouse racks, stock status instead of “used or damaged.”

Private by design
Everything stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no cloud sync, and no grocery feed. Open the app offline and the same shelves are still there.

Who it is for
Home cooks who buy in bulk, households that share a pantry, anyone tired of buying a third bottle of soy sauce. Start with one drawer. Grow it into a full kitchen map.

Larder Haven — know the shelf before you write the list.
