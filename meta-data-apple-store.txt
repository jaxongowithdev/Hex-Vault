APP NAME
Canister Press

SUBTITLE
Map your film rolls

KEYWORDS
film,35mm,roll,camera,darkroom,analog,canister,stock,photo,offline

PROMOTION
Log cans, file rolls, and find any stock before you load. Private, offline.

DESCRIPTION
Canister Press is a darkroom catalog for analog shooters who already own more film than they can keep in their head.

Name every can in the fridge — the Portra tin, the travel pouch, the expired-box drawer — then file each roll where it actually lives. When you wonder if that last HP5 is still in the bag, search once instead of emptying three tins.

What you can do
• Create named cans with a mark, room, and rack or slot
• File stock by format: 35mm, 120, instant, sheet, plus cameras, lenses, chemistry, paper, and filters
• Mark state as Unshot, Loaded, Developed, or Expired
• Attach a box photo from the camera or your album
• Bookmark the rolls you want in the next camera
• Move a roll from one can to another and keep a short note
• See a stock snapshot: how many cans are in use, which formats run deepest
• Export or restore the whole list as a JSON file

Why it is different
Canister Press is not a camera remote and not a generic storage box. It is a lab map. The language, layout, and states are written for film shooters: cans instead of bins, rolls instead of items, lab notes instead of packing labels.

Private by design
Everything stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no cloud sync, and no film shop. Open the app in the darkroom with no signal and the same cans are still there.

Who it is for
Weekend 35mm shooters, medium-format travelers, anyone tired of buying a third brick of the same stock.

Canister Press — know the can before you load.
