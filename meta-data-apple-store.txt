APP NAME
Reel Bay

SUBTITLE
Map your tackle trays

KEYWORDS
fishing,tackle,lure,hook,reel,fly,bay,gear,rod,tray,jig,offline

PROMOTION
Stage trays, log lures, and find any rig before you launch. Private, offline.

DESCRIPTION
Reel Bay is a tackle map for anglers who already own more lures than they can keep in their head.

Name every tray in the boat — the jig box, the fly book, the spare-leader tin — then file each lure where it actually lives. When you wonder if that gold spoon is still in the dock bag, search once instead of emptying three compartments.

What you can do
• Create named bays with a mark, dock, and tray slot
• File gear by class: lures, flies, line, hooks, sinkers, rods, reels, tools, and apparel
• Mark condition as Fresh, Fished, Rusted, or Spare
• Attach a photo from the camera or your album
• Bookmark the lures that always go in the boat first
• Move a lure from one bay to another and keep a short note
• See a kit snapshot: how many bays are in use, which classes run deepest
• Export or restore the whole kit as a JSON file

Why it is different
Reel Bay is not a fish-finder and not a generic storage box. It is a dock-side catalog. The language, layout, and states are written for anglers: bays instead of bins, lures instead of items, water notes instead of packing labels.

Private by design
Everything stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no cloud sync, and no tackle shop. Open the app at the ramp with no signal and the same trays are still there.

Who it is for
Weekend bass anglers, fly fishers with more than one book, anyone tired of buying a third pack of the same jig.

Reel Bay — know the tray before you launch.
