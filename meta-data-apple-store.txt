APP NAME
Stash Loom

SUBTITLE
Map your yarn stash

KEYWORDS
yarn,knit,stash,skein,wool,fiber,basket,craft,merino,pattern,offline,studio

PROMOTION
Map baskets, log skeins, and find any fiber in the studio. A private yarn catalog — fully offline.

DESCRIPTION
Stash Loom is a quiet catalog for knitters and crocheters who already own more yarn than they remember.

Map every basket in the studio — the sock leftovers, the sweater wool, the notions tin — then file each skein where it actually lives. When you wonder whether you still have that merino dye lot, search once instead of emptying three drawers.

What you can do
• Create named baskets with a tag, studio corner, and drawer or hook
• File skeins by fiber: wool, cotton, alpaca, silk, blends, plus tools, patterns, and notions
• Mark state as New, Wound, Partial, or Scrap
• Attach a fiber photo from the camera or your album
• Pin the skeins on your current project
• Rewind a skein from one basket to another and keep a short note
• See a stash snapshot: how many baskets are in use, which fibers run deepest
• Export or restore the whole stash as a JSON file

Why it is different
Stash Loom is not a shopping list and not a generic storage box. It is a studio map. The language, layout, and states are written for makers: baskets instead of bins, skeins instead of items, dye lots and project notes instead of packing labels.

Private by design
Everything stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no cloud sync, and no yarn marketplace. Open the app offline and the same baskets are still there.

Who it is for
New knitters building a first stash, long-time makers with more than one closet, anyone tired of buying a third ball of the same sock yarn.

Stash Loom — know the basket before you cast on.
